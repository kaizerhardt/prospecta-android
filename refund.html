Refund & Credit Policy

Draft product policy · Updated 2 October 2026
Pre-launch legal review required. This draft defines intended product behavior and is not legal advice. Replace placeholders and have qualified counsel review the final policy for your operating company and launch markets.

Subscriptions

Subscription renewals, cancellation timing, trial terms, taxes, and store-specific rules should be shown before purchase. Purchases made through Apple or Google may be subject to those stores’ billing and refund processes.

Credits

Prospecta should charge credits only for clearly defined billable actions. Where the product promises “usable results,” failed enrichment or verification attempts should not be charged unless disclosed otherwise.

Top-ups

Purchased top-up credits may have different expiry or rollover treatment from monthly included credits. The exact rule should be displayed at checkout and in the account ledger.

Refund requests

Production support should provide a documented review process for duplicate charges, technical failures, unauthorized purchases, and legally required refunds.

Acceptable Use Policy

Draft product policy · Updated 2 October 2026
Pre-launch legal review required. This draft defines intended product behavior and is not legal advice. Replace placeholders and have qualified counsel review the final policy for your operating company and launch markets.

Allowed use

Legitimate business prospecting, market research, sales outreach, recruitment, partnerships, and customer-development activity using information the user is permitted to process.

Prohibited use

  • Harassment, threats, deceptive identity, phishing, fraud, or malware.
  • Ignoring unsubscribe, objection, suppression, or do-not-contact requests.
  • Targeting based on highly sensitive traits or unlawfully obtained personal information.
  • Bypassing access controls, CAPTCHAs, rate limits, authentication, or terms-enforced technical restrictions.
  • Purchasing or uploading stolen, breached, or clearly unauthorized datasets.
  • Sending content that violates applicable law or connected-provider policies.

Enforcement

Prospecta should maintain automated and manual controls including send limits, bounce thresholds, abuse reports, suppression lists, account review, and suspension.

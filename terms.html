Prospecta provides business prospecting, research, messaging, sending, workflow, and analytics tools. Results are probabilistic and users remain responsible for reviewing outputs and deciding whether to contact a prospect.

Users must provide accurate account information, safeguard credentials, and promptly report unauthorized use. Workspace administrators are responsible for authorized users and connected accounts.

3. Credits and subscriptions

Credits may be required for enrichment, verification, research, generation, unlocking, or sending. The product should show the expected charge before a paid action. Failed operations should be handled according to the published credit policy.

4. User responsibilities

Users must comply with applicable privacy, anti-spam, direct-marketing, consumer, intellectual-property, platform, and sector-specific rules. Prospecta must not be used to impersonate others, mislead recipients, evade opt-outs, or conduct abusive campaigns.

5. Third-party services

Connected providers may have their own terms, limits, and availability. Prospecta cannot guarantee uninterrupted operation of third-party services.

6. Suspension

Accounts may be limited or suspended for abuse, excessive bounce rates, unlawful activity, security risk, or repeated violations of acceptable-use rules.

7. Disclaimers and liability

Production terms should define warranties, service-level commitments if any, liability caps, indemnities, governing law, and dispute-resolution terms with qualified counsel.

Data Processing Addendum

Draft product policy · Updated 2 October 2026
Pre-launch legal review required. This draft defines intended product behavior and is not legal advice. Replace placeholders and have qualified counsel review the final policy for your operating company and launch markets.

Scope

This draft is a product placeholder for business customers that require controller/processor terms. The final DPA must be prepared for the actual operating company, hosting region, subprocessors, and product architecture.

Security

Expected controls include encryption in transit, encryption at rest where appropriate, least-privilege access, secrets management, audit logging, incident response, deletion workflows, and access reviews.

Subprocessors

Maintain a current subprocessor list covering hosting, authentication, email, AI/model providers, payment processors, verification vendors, analytics, support, and monitoring.

International transfers

Use legally appropriate transfer mechanisms where required and document data locations and subprocessors accurately.

Outreach Compliance Guidelines

Draft product policy · Updated 2 October 2026
Pre-launch legal review required. This draft defines intended product behavior and is not legal advice. Replace placeholders and have qualified counsel review the final policy for your operating company and launch markets.

1. No single global cold-email rule

Commercial outreach laws differ by country and sometimes by recipient type. The production system should apply location-aware defaults rather than assuming that one consent model is valid worldwide.

2. Business-purpose relevance

Only contact prospects where there is a reasonable business connection between the prospect and the user’s offer. Messages should identify the sender and avoid misleading claims.

3. Opt-out and suppression

Every applicable campaign should include a practical opt-out method. Once a recipient opts out or objects, Prospecta should suppress that address or entity from future outreach for the relevant workspace.

4. Deliverability safeguards

Use per-mailbox send limits, bounce-rate monitoring, duplicate suppression, complaint monitoring, and automatic campaign pauses. Do not encourage users to evade Gmail, Microsoft, ISP, or anti-abuse controls.

5. Public business data is not automatically unrestricted

The fact that contact information is visible online does not by itself resolve every privacy, direct-marketing, database, or platform-rule issue. Source, purpose, jurisdiction, and recipient rights still matter.

QUICK HELP

Common issues

Why did a lead bounce?

Publicly listed addresses can become stale. Prospecta separates “found publicly” from actual send/no-bounce status.

Why was sending paused?

Safety rules can pause a campaign when bounce or account-health thresholds are exceeded.

Where are my credits?

Web, iOS and Android purchases are reconciled to one server-side credit ledger.

Can I request deletion?

Yes. Privacy requests are handled from Legal & safety or through support.

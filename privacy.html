Privacy Policy

Draft product policy · Updated 2 October 2026
Pre-launch legal review required. This draft defines intended product behavior and is not legal advice. Replace placeholders and have qualified counsel review the final policy for your operating company and launch markets.

1. What Prospecta processes

Prospecta may process account information, workspace settings, connected-mailbox metadata, business prospect information, campaign activity, payment records, support requests, device and security logs, and user-supplied business content.

2. Business prospect data

The product is intended to use business information from public or properly licensed sources. Users must not use Prospecta to target people using sensitive personal data, private credentials, unlawfully obtained datasets, or information that they are not permitted to process.

3. Why data is processed

Data is used to operate the service, find and qualify business prospects, generate requested outreach, prevent duplicates, reconcile sends and bounces, provide support, maintain security, process payments, and improve product reliability.

4. Connected services

Where a user connects services such as Gmail, Google Calendar, payment providers, email-verification providers, or data suppliers, Prospecta should request only the permissions required for the selected feature and should explain those permissions at connection time.

5. Retention and deletion

Production deployment should define retention periods by data type and provide account export and deletion flows. Suppression records may need to be retained in a limited form to prevent re-contact after an opt-out.

6. International transfers

Cloud and subprocessors may process data in multiple countries. The production business should use appropriate contractual and technical safeguards for international transfers where required.

7. Your rights

Depending on location, users and data subjects may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object, or receive a copy of personal data. Requests should be routed to the designated privacy contact.

8. Singapore and other jurisdictions

If Prospecta is operated from Singapore, the production policy should address the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). If users or prospects are in the EEA/UK, the product may also need GDPR/UK GDPR processes; other markets may impose additional privacy, marketing, and data-broker requirements.
